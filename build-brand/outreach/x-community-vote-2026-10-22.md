→ Alternating: one week full buy & burn, next week full USDC prizes, repeat
→ Hybrid: every week, the budget is split between burning and prizes

Prizes are a draw, not a return. $NOAH is a memecoin with no claim on the platform; a smaller supply sets no price. Not investment advice.
