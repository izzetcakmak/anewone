How it works
  • Every launch (TokenCreated) and every trade (Trade) event on any ANEWONE platform deployment (current v6 · v5) counts as 1 action — a launch with a starting buy counts as both. Contract upgrades never cost you progress. Testnet is free — gas comes from the Circle faucet.
  • Snapshot window: every action since the current platform deployment (July 19, 2026) counts — early supporters included — until the moment Arc mainnet goes live, when the cutoff freezes and the final snapshot is published here in full. Campaign announced July 28, 2026.
  • Wallets ranked by total transactions; ties go to the earlier wallet. Team wallets are listed but excluded from ranking.
  • The top 33 wallets are entered into a decentralized raffle tool the same day. Winners receive their $NOAH on Arc mainnet; results are published here and on X.
  • Everything is open source: github.com/izzetcakmak/anewone (snapshot engine: monitor/snapshot.mjs). Questions: support@anewone.xyz
