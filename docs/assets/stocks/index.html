How a stock token works

1 · THE TOKENOne token, one shareBacked buys the share and keeps it with a regulated custodian in a segregated account; the token is a tracker certificate on it, redeemable with the issuer. Proof of reserves is published per stock and shown on each card. No voting rights; dividends are reinvested into the token's balance.
2 · ON ARCTrade in USDC, 24/7The tokens are plain ERC-20s, so on Arc they trade in Uniswap pools against USDC from any wallet, at any hour. The pool price can stray from the exchange price when the market is closed; both are on the card, and the quote shows the difference before you sign.
3 · THE WRAPPERWhy you receive wTSLAx, not TSLAxDividends and splits change xStock balances automatically. Pools cannot hold a token whose balances move, so they hold the wrapped, non-rebasing version (ERC-4626). It is worth exactly the same share; the card shows your holding either way and can wrap or unwrap in one click.
