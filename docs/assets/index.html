How an order works

1 · ELIGIBILITYThe issuer whitelists youAnemoy checks who you are and marks your wallet as a member of the fund on Arc. The card shows "whitelisted" the moment that lands; nothing on this site can skip it.
2 · DEPOSITRequest, then claimYou send USDC on Arc with a deposit request. The fund's hub on Ethereum approves orders daily and sets the price; your shares then wait in the vault until you claim them, from here or from Centrifuge.
3 · REDEEMThe same, in reverseA redeem request locks your shares; once the issuer processes it, USDC waits for you to claim. Each request carries a message to Ethereum, and that message fee, paid in USDC on Arc, shows in the quote before you sign.

Fund data is read from the Arc chain and from Centrifuge's indexer and the issuer's published factsheets; yields are the issuer's trailing 30-day figures, variable, and no promise of future returns.
