About

A NEW ONE is a memecoin launchpad on Arc, Circle's stablecoin network. It is built in the open by one developer, and everything it runs on is public: the contracts, the site and the jobs behind them.

How the project works in the open

  • The contracts, the site and the indexing jobs are open source under the MIT license: github.com/izzetcakmak/anewone.
  • The site is static. There is no backend, no accounts and no tracking: see the privacy page.
  • Curve reserves can never be withdrawn, and at graduation they move into a Uniswap v3 position the platform contract holds for good. The mechanics are spelled out in the docs.
  • The contracts are small and covered by 83 tests, including migrations run against the Uniswap v3 contracts Uniswap itself published. Read the code before you trust it: that is why it is public.
  • Security reports are welcome: security.txt.

What we will never do

A NEW ONE will never:
  • ask for your seed phrase, private key or password, anywhere, for any reason;
  • message you first on Telegram, Discord or X to offer support, an airdrop or a whitelist;
  • ask you to "verify", "sync" or "migrate" your wallet on another site;
  • ask you to send funds to an address to receive more back.
