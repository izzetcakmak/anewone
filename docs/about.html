About A NEW ONE

A NEW ONE is the launch and distribution front end for real-world assets on Arc, Circle's stablecoin network: funds and credit on Centrifuge rails, stocks from licensed issuers, USDC that earns and USDC you can borrow on Morpho. The issuer is always a regulated entity; we never issue anything ourselves. It is built in the open by one developer, and everything it runs on is public: the contracts, the site and the jobs behind them.

What we are

Vision: real-world assets on Arc, for anyone with a wallet, settled in USDC around the clock. Mission: to be the interface, never the issuer: one front door where a tokenized fund, a credit pool, a stock or a yield vault is launched, found and bought, with the issuer, the terms and the on-chain data in plain sight. Three rules hold everywhere: we never custody, we never issue, we never advise. The memecoin launchpad we started with lives on as AnewOne.Fun.

How the project works in the open

  • The contracts, the site and the indexing jobs are open source under the MIT license: github.com/izzetcakmak/anewone.
  • The launchpad runs in your browser against the chain. There are no accounts and no tracking; the few server functions (the RPC relay, Deck Hand, card purchases and bridge quotes) are each described on the privacy page.
  • Curve reserves can never be withdrawn, and at graduation they move into a Uniswap v3 position the platform contract holds for good. The mechanics are spelled out in the docs.
  • The contracts are small and covered by 87 tests, including migrations run against the Uniswap v3 contracts Uniswap itself published and a rehearsal on a fork of Arc mainnet against the live platform. There has been no external audit yet. Read the code before you trust it: that is why it is public.
  • Security reports are welcome: security.txt.

What we will never do

A NEW ONE will never:
  • ask for your seed phrase, private key or password, anywhere, for any reason;
  • message you first on Telegram, Discord or X to offer support, an airdrop or a whitelist;
  • ask you to "verify", "sync" or "migrate" your wallet on another site;
  • ask you to send funds to an address to receive more back.

Anyone doing this in our name is an impostor. The only addresses we use:
