What A NEW ONE is

A NEW ONE is a website that lets you use open-source smart contracts on the Arc network to launch and trade tokens. It is non-custodial: we never hold your funds or your keys, and every transaction is created, shown and approved in your own wallet. The contracts are public and their source is on GitHub.

Risk

Memecoins are highly speculative. Prices can fall to zero and you can lose everything you put in. Nothing on this website is financial, investment, legal or tax advice. Tokens on testnet have no value.

Token prices are set by each token's bonding curve and, after graduation, by its Uniswap pool. We do not set, guarantee or support any price.

We may stop showing any token on this website, for example one that impersonates others or breaks the law. That changes only what this website displays; it does not change the token or anyone's balance on chain.

Fees

Every trade on a curve pays a 1% fee: half to the token's creator, who can collect it within a 7-day window, and half to the platform. After graduation the Uniswap pool charges 1% as well, split the same way on its USDC side. Network gas is paid separately, in USDC. The details are in the Docs.
