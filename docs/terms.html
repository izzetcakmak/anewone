What A NEW ONE is

A NEW ONE is a website that lets you use open-source smart contracts on the Arc network to launch and trade tokens. It is non-custodial: we never hold your funds or your keys, and every transaction is created, shown and approved in your own wallet. The contracts are public and their source is on GitHub.

Token prices are set by each token's bonding curve and, after graduation, by its Uniswap pool. We do not set, guarantee or support any price.

Fees

Every trade on a curve pays a 1.5% fee: 0.5% to the token's creator and 1% to the platform. A creator's fees collect in a pot that must be claimed within 7 days of the pot starting to accrue. A creator who claims before the deadline shown in the app keeps the full pot; a pot left unclaimed past that window goes to the platform, and the creator's next fee starts a fresh pot with a fresh 7 days. After graduation the Uniswap pool charges its own 1% fee tier, whose USDC side is split between creator and platform in the same proportion. Network gas is paid separately, in USDC. The details are in the Docs.
