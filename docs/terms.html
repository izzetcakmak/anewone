Fees

Earn: vault performance and management fees go to the vault's curator, none to us. Borrow: when a new loan is opened, an origination fee of 0.15% (15 basis points) of the amount borrowed is withheld from the proceeds; 90% of it goes to us and 10% to Arc. It is shown in the quote before you sign, applies only at origination, and is not charged on repayments, added collateral or later draws. Interest goes to the market's lenders under the Morpho protocol. Swap & Bridge: non-USDC payments carry a 0.25% integrator fee on LI.FI routes. AnewOne.Fun: every trade on a curve pays a 1.5% fee: 0.5% to the token's creator and 1% to the platform. A creator's fees collect in a pot that must be claimed within 7 days of the pot starting to accrue. A creator who claims before the deadline shown in the app keeps the full pot; a pot left unclaimed past that window goes to the platform, and the creator's next fee starts a fresh pot with a fresh 7 days. After graduation the Uniswap pool charges its own 1% fee tier, whose USDC side is split between creator and platform in the same proportion. Network gas is paid separately, in USDC. The details are in the Docs.
