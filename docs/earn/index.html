Yields are variable, set by the market, and shown as reported by the vault; past rates do not predict future ones. Vaults carry smart-contract and credit risk, including loss of principal, and a vault marked low liquidity may not let you withdraw at once. Vault fees go to the curator, none to us. A loan is over-collateralised: if the collateral's price falls until the health factor reaches 1.0, Morpho liquidates part of it, with a penalty. Borrow rates are variable. A NEW ONE may take an origination fee on a new loan, shown in the quote before you sign. Before you start

One signature, once per wallet. It costs nothing and moves nothing; it only records that you accept the terms.

  • A NEW ONE is a non-custodial interface: it never holds your funds or keys, never issues an asset and never gives advice.
  • Vaults and markets are run by third parties on Morpho. Yields are variable and loans can be liquidated without notice. You can lose everything you put in.
  • Fees are shown before you sign: none on Earn, 0.15% on a new loan.
  • You are 18 or older, act for yourself, and are responsible for your own taxes and local rules.
  • You accept the Terms of Use and the Privacy Notice.
