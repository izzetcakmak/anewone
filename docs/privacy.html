Privacy

A NEW ONE is a static website with no backend of its own. There are no accounts, we do not collect your personal data, and we will never ask for your seed phrase or private key.

Last updated: 11 September 2026

What we do not collect

  • No sign-up, no user accounts, no profiles.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking scripts, and no tracking cookies.
  • No server of ours stores what you do on the site. The site is a set of static files.

What stays in your browser

To remember your settings and load faster, the site keeps a few items in your browser's local storage: your colour theme, the wallet you last connected, your watchlist, and cached copies of public chain data and token images. These never leave your device and are never sent to us. You can remove them at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Public blockchain data

Launching a token, trading and posting a comment are transactions on the Arc network. Like all blockchain transactions they are public and permanent, and they are linked to your wallet address. Nobody, including us, can edit or delete them.

Services your browser talks to

Using the site means your browser connects to a few outside services, each under its own privacy policy:

  • Hosting: the site is served by Vercel, which keeps standard request logs.
  • Arc network providers: your browser reads chain data from Arc RPC endpoints (such as Arc's public RPC, QuickNode, Blockdaemon and dRPC), which see those requests.
  • Fonts: typefaces load from Google Fonts.
  • Your wallet: MetaMask, OKX, Rabby or any wallet you connect handles your keys and transactions under its own policy.
  • Continue with Google: if you choose it, sign-in is handled by Web3Auth under its own privacy policy. It creates a non-custodial wallet; we never see your Google password.

Your keys stay yours

A NEW ONE never asks for a seed phrase, a private key or a password. Every transaction is shown in your own wallet and needs your approval. Anyone asking for your keys in our name is a scam.
