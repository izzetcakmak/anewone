How the trip goes

  1. Pick a chain and what you pay with. USDC goes straight to the bridge. Anything else is swapped into USDC first by LI.FI, on the chain you are on.
  2. One bridge signature. Circle's CCTP burns the USDC and its Forwarding Service mints it on Arc for you. You never need gas on Arc.
  3. Buy on the floor. If you picked a coin, the bridged USDC buys it on its bonding curve as soon as it lands; one more signature, on Arc.

Chains that can board

Base, Ethereum, Arbitrum, OP Mainnet, Polygon, Avalanche, Unichain, Linea, World Chain, Sonic, Monad, Sei, HyperEVM, Ink. Fast transfers arrive in about 20 seconds where Circle offers them.

Fees, plainly

Circle takes a few basis points on fast transfers (none on standard) plus about 0.02 USDC to mint on Arc for you. LI.FI swaps carry the pool's price and a fixed fee of a cent or two; on mainnet 0.25% of a non-USDC payment goes to the floor, shown as its own line. The quote shows everything before you sign, and the amount bridged after a swap is what actually arrived in your wallet, not an estimate.
